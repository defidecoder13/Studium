# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Studium is primarily for **self-learners** — professionals and independent learners working through dense material on their own time, often on the document that is currently blocking them. They arrive skeptical (burned by hallucinated summaries) and convert on proof, not promises.

A secondary audience of university students and academic researchers shares the same job — exam prep, seminar defense — and the same demand: every claim traceable to a page.

## Product Purpose

Studium turns textbooks, papers, and lectures into cited answers, chapter quizzes, and flashcards without ever losing the source page. It exists because AI study tools hallucinate, and learners cannot defend what they cannot verify. Success is a visitor who uploads a document, taps a citation, watches it open the exact page — and stays.

## Positioning

Every AI answer cites the exact page it came from. If it is not on a page, Studium does not claim it.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: **Start learning — free** (hero and closing section). Secondary fallback for skeptics: **See how it reads** (scrolls to the reader proof).
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: *Master any subject with precision.*
- Belief ladder: citations are real and tappable → it works on my material (any subject, PDF or YouTube) → getting started costs nothing and needs no credit card.
- Proof on hand: the Alex Rivera verbatim quote with verifiable metadata (Stanford, History of Science PhD, since Dec 2025, 412 cards, 94% retention) in `app/page.tsx`; the four-domain manuscript strip (Medicine, Law, Physics, History with page counts); the live Fig. 1 reader mock with page-anchored note.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, scholarly. Voice is editorial and confident: short declarative claims, mono provenance labels, zero hype. The tool speaks like a careful colleague — specific numbers, named pages, no superlatives it cannot point at.

## Anti-references

Generic AI SaaS: gradient heroes and gradient text, big-number metric templates, glassmorphism cards, pulsing baits, and three-identical-card feature grids. Anything that reads "AI made this" at a glance is a failure, even when the underlying layout is conventional.

## Design Principles

1. **Provenance before prose.** Every claim earns its place by pointing at a source; design makes citations scannable, never decorative.
2. **Show the mechanism, not the magic.** Chunking, page numbers, and honest limits are on the surface — the product teaches how it works as it sells.
3. **Restraint is the brand.** One indigo accent on neutral canvas, hairline cadence, flat borders. Decoration must justify itself or leave.
4. **Skeptics convert on specifics.** Vague filler ("advanced language models", "all domains") loses to exact numbers, named pages, and verifiable metadata everywhere.
5. **No fake UI, ever.** Limits stated before commitment, no dead buttons, no stub actions, no invented data — trust is the product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1, keyboard-operable surfaces with visible focus, honest motion (no decorative pulse), and real labels on every control.
