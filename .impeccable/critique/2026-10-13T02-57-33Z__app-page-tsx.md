## Overall Impression

Gut reaction: a landing page with genuine voice and a real moat (page-anchored provenance as a design material) wrapped around an app interior that drifts toward template SaaS — card grids, lift hovers, native confirms. Biggest opportunity: make the highest-stakes moments (deletes, uploads) feel as considered as the landing. Trust is the product; confirm() destroys it.

Emotional journey: landing arc is strong (pulsing provenance badge → Fig.1 paper peak → confident inverted CTA close). App sags at upload anxiety (content-free "Indexing document vectors...") and quiz completion (🎉 breaks register; weak-topic box rescues it — keep box, drop emoji).

## What's Working

1. **Provenance as a design material.** Inline [Page 12] chips that scroll the PDF, P.12 pills, source lines on flashcards/quizzes/review. Display-serif (claims) / Sans (body) / Mono (provenance) trichotomy makes citations scannable. This is the moat and it reads as one.
2. **Editorial restraint on landing.** Single indigo #4f46e5 on neutral canvas, ink-night dark, hairline rules, Fig.1 caption. Contrast verified: #52525b on #fafafa = 7.41:1, #a1a1aa on #131316 = 7.24:1, emerald chips pass both modes.
3. **Complete, kind state design.** Skeletons everywhere, empties with next actions, calm escapable errors; bookmarks DB failure degrades to empty state instead of crashing.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Destructive actions have zero reassurance.** Every irreversible action is native confirm() (library:169, flashcards:91, quizzes:73, planner:148, settings:150,165-166). No scope summary, no undo. Upload failures use alert(). Fix: inline destructive dialog (scope summary + type-to-confirm for clear-all/delete-account + Undo toast); surface limits before commit (4.5MB/50pp buried at library:701-703). Suggested: /impeccable harden.

**[P1] Landing/app visual language forks.** Radius, elevation, type, active-tokens diverge (landing rounded-xl + border-only; app rounded-2xl/3xl + hover:shadow-lg hover:-translate-y-0.5). Fix: lock one scale (xl max cards, lg controls), kill lift-shadows, unify active-token to bg-primary/10 + rail. Suggested: /impeccable polish.

**[P1] Dark-mode small primary text fails contrast.** #6366f1 on #131316 = 4.15:1, on #0b0b0d = 4.40:1 — below 4.5:1 for the 10–11px mono accents (DUE, [P.12], citation chips). Fix: render small primary text with --accent #818cf8 in dark. Suggested: /impeccable audit.

**[P2] Reader split-view collapses gracelessly on small screens.** Panes stack full-width below lg with no switcher; page navigator and bookmark hide on mobile; hover-only page badge is touch-dead. Fix: segmented view switcher below lg + sticky mini page-nav. Suggested: /impeccable adapt.

**[P2] Quiz + review tone breaks the editorial contract.** "Assessment Completed! 🎉", 💡 tip, ⭐/⚡/📑 elsewhere. Review ratings never teach honest grading (core SM-2 behavior). Fix: strip emoji to mono/serif voice; add grader coach line; rename "Quit Assessment" honestly. Suggested: /impeccable clarify then /impeccable polish.
